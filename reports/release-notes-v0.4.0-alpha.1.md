# NOESIS v0.4.0-alpha.1 release notes

**Status:** local candidate preparation, not released.
**Candidate:** `c45f4d6`

## Implemented

- Snooze, Nap, Dream, and Deep Sleep bounded levels.
- Consolidate, associate, imagine, rehearse, and reframe concrete modes.
- Unique `mixed` expansion with independent scope and focus.
- Durable budget, progress, pause/resume, cancellation, and hypothesis outputs.
- Retrospection over authorized observations and outcomes.
- Held-out policy evaluation with overlap rejection and rollback identity.
- Reverie dashboard controls and inspectable outputs.

## Validation

`npm test` passed 52 tests. Authenticated Reverie journeys passed queue, bounded run, hypothesis output, cancellation, and retrospection. Headless Chromium rendered desktop and mobile Observatory views.

## Open gates

- Provider-backed model calls, embeddings, and real experiments are not enabled.
- Interactive browser control was not run; HTTP journeys cover the mutations.
- Physical second-device LAN validation remains an M8 check.
- M5 Lore integration remains next.

No package was published, deployed, or exposed to the public Internet.
