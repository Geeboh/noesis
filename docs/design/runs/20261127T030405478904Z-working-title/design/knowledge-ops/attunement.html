Evidence meaning

Stated and inferred preferences are not interchangeable. Compliance, silence, and repeated turns in one incident are excluded as independent confirmation.

Safe correction

Correction is reversible through a compensating revision. It changes application scope, not source history. No correction grants permission for external actions.
