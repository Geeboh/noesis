Meaning boundaries

Ebb is reversible visibility management. It does not change truth, confidence, validity, retention, or source evidence. Explicit forgetting is a separate mutation.

Protected guidance

Applicable explicit constraints, open commitments, and manually pinned records are protected from ordinary fading. Revival preserves counterevidence.
