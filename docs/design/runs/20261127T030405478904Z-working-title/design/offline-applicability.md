# Offline applicability

Offline product operation is `not-required` for this resolved NOESIS scope. The product is local-first and LAN-served, but the source requirements do not require a browser to operate without the local service. The applicable check is network failure and recovery, recorded in `final-quality-evidence.md`.

This disposition does not claim cold offline readiness, WAN-disabled operation, service-worker behavior, or offline data access.
